# Simple training policies selected by the owner — 19 October 2026

The owner relayed the trainer permission to choose simple defaults on 19 October 2026. See TRAINER_UPDATE_2026_10_19_AR.md. These are training settings, not statutory HR entitlements. Only Annual, Sick and Unpaid are offered; requests contain dates only, with no hourly permissions.

- Business timezone: Attendance:BusinessTimeZoneId, default Jordan Standard Time. Stored timestamps are UTC; dashboard dates and decision date-range boundaries use the business clock. Late threshold: Attendance:LateAfterLocalTime (09:15 default), with no per-shift schedule.
- Attendance work date is the local check-in date. Multiple completed sessions per employee/work date are allowed; at most one open shift across all dates. Checkout can close a prior-date open record. Maximum interval 24 hours. Longer/stale records need authorized manual correction. The manual screen has an explicit check-out date; blank means the work date. Choose a particular session from the attendance list when several sessions exist. It does not guess that an earlier clock time means tomorrow. UTC-only and no future timestamps. Hours rounded to 2 decimals. Missing checkout remains open with zero hours; no automatic absence inference without a work calendar.
- Department manager belongs to the managed department and must be active when assigned. Create the department, assign employees, then select its manager. Transfer of a department manager requires reassignment first. This preserves a single-department authorization scope; confirm if multi-department managers are needed.
- Active employee lists default to Active (legacy Arabic status values are supported). Explicit status filters and authorized reports expose inactive records. Deleted records require the restoration workflow. Inactive/deleted linked employees cannot sign in and are rejected when existing cookies are validated. Reactivation/restoration can restore eligibility; no permanent session-revocation policy is claimed.
- Leave dates are inclusive calendar days. Pending/approved overlaps are rejected in a Serializable transaction. Reviewed requests cannot be reviewed/cancelled again. Department managers cannot approve their own request. HR roles retain existing organization-wide decision authority. Pending requests reserve inclusive calendar days, approval converts reserved days to used days without another deduction, and rejection/cancellation release them. New requests do not carry times; historical time columns remain for compatibility, without being displayed in leave pages; remaining balances are clamped at zero and submission is not blocked solely by an exceeded allowance. Leave allowances are configured defaults; no approved accrual, carryover, weekends/holidays or partial-day scheme was supplied. These conditional policies require business approval before enforcement.
- Employee numbers remain reserved after deletion. Position changes are audited and do not delete referenced employees. Audit history is append-only at the application layer. Production database permissions/backups must separately prevent privileged tampering.
- Personal contact changes record field name only. Passwords/tokens/secrets are prohibited in audit values.

## Metrics

Active headcount: undeleted employees with active status. Department total: undeleted employees; inactive count: total minus active. New hires: undeleted employees with HireDate inside the inclusive selected date range. Status groups: undeleted employees grouped by stored status. Pending leaves: undeleted employees' Pending requests. Attendance summary: recorded rows in selected work-date range, counts by status and sum of TotalHours; not inferred absences. Decisions: approved/rejected records whose review timestamp is inside the business-date interval. Recent activity: latest 10 audit events only for audit-authorized roles.
